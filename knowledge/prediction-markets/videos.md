# Videos regarding prediction markets

## Theoretical Foundations & History

* [Betting on What Happens Next | Prediction Markets Explained | In The Money by Zerodha](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2bvAea2hqR8): This video covers the academic foundations of event contracts. It traces the theory from Friedrich Hayek's "The Use of Knowledge in Society" to Kenneth Arrow’s state-contingent securities and Eugene Fama's Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH). Crucially for a modeler, it introduces Robin Hanson's Logarithmic Market Scoring Rule (LMSR)—the core mathematical mechanism used by automated market makers (AMMs) to price binary options and maintain continuous liquidity.


## Trading Frameworks & Finding "Edge"

* [ My Prediction Market Trading Playbook (5-Step Framework)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UWoXBLAXHEY): A highly analytical breakdown of how to treat prediction markets like real derivative trading rather than gambling. It details how to mathematically isolate mispriced probabilities in binary contracts ($0.01 to $1.00 shares) and account for transaction friction and risk premiums.
* [ How the World’s #1 Prediction Markets Trader Finds Edge! - Domer on Trading Global Political Events](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TJpXnvFuvZg): Odds on Open with legendary trader "Domer". It contrasts prediction market strategy against traditional equities. You will learn how information shocks reprice event contracts instantaneously and how to models fast-arriving, unstructured news signals.
* [ How to Find Your Edge on Prediction Markets [Beginner-Strategy]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=17Xdp1eGDLI): Focuses on the data sources you can scrape to feed an algorithmic model. It highlights quantitative data tools (like Eventquant's Mention Miner or PredictShark.io) used to track real-time money flows and textual frequency analysis to predict political and macroeconomic outcomes.
