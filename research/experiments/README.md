# Experiments

## What belongs here
- Materials specific to experiments work.
- Clearly scoped research artifacts and references.

## Naming conventions
- Use `kebab-case` filenames.
- Use `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md` for date-based entries.
- Use `topic-short-title.md` for evergreen entries.

## How findings should be documented
- Record question, method, evidence, and conclusion.
- Include confidence level, limitations, and next steps.
- Link related hypotheses, experiments, and summaries.
