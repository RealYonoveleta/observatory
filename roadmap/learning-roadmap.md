# Learning Roadmap

## 1. Prediction Markets
- Goals: Build foundational understanding of market design and probability interpretation.
- Topics: Contract types, resolution, liquidity, efficiency.
- Suggested books: *The Signal and the Noise* (Silver), *Superforecasting* (Tetlock & Gardner).
- Suggested courses: Intro market microstructure or forecasting seminars.
- Success criteria: Can explain market mechanics and evaluate price-as-probability assumptions.

## 2. Probability
- Goals: Develop rigorous probabilistic reasoning.
- Topics: Random variables, conditional probability, Bayes theorem.
- Suggested books: *Probability Theory: The Logic of Science* (Jaynes).
- Suggested courses: University probability fundamentals.
- Success criteria: Correctly frame uncertainty with coherent probabilistic models.

## 3. Statistics
- Goals: Build inferential and empirical analysis skills.
- Topics: Estimation, hypothesis testing, uncertainty quantification.
- Suggested books: *Statistical Inference* (Casella & Berger).
- Suggested courses: Mathematical statistics sequence.
- Success criteria: Can design and critique empirical tests with clear assumptions.

## 4. Forecasting
- Goals: Learn structured forecasting methodology.
- Topics: Proper scoring rules, calibration, decomposition.
- Suggested books: *Forecasting: Principles and Practice* (Hyndman & Athanasopoulos).
- Suggested courses: Forecasting and decision analysis courses.
- Success criteria: Produces calibrated forecasts and evaluates them robustly.

## 5. Economics
- Goals: Understand incentives, equilibria, and market behavior.
- Topics: Micro foundations, game theory, welfare.
- Suggested books: *Microeconomic Theory* (Mas-Colell et al.).
- Suggested courses: Intermediate microeconomics.
- Success criteria: Applies incentive analysis to prediction-market design.

## 6. Behavioral Economics
- Goals: Incorporate cognitive and behavioral effects.
- Topics: Biases, heuristics, bounded rationality.
- Suggested books: *Thinking, Fast and Slow* (Kahneman).
- Suggested courses: Behavioral economics survey.
- Success criteria: Identifies likely behavioral distortions in forecasts and markets.

## 7. Market Microstructure
- Goals: Understand order flow and price formation.
- Topics: Order books, spreads, adverse selection.
- Suggested books: *Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading* (Cartea et al.).
- Suggested courses: Financial market microstructure.
- Success criteria: Interprets microstructure dynamics in event markets.

## 8. Monte Carlo
- Goals: Build simulation-based reasoning under uncertainty.
- Topics: Sampling, scenario analysis, convergence.
- Suggested books: *Monte Carlo Methods in Financial Engineering* (Glasserman).
- Suggested courses: Numerical methods and simulation.
- Success criteria: Designs robust simulation studies for decision support.

## 9. NLP
- Goals: Extract signals from unstructured text.
- Topics: Representation, classification, information extraction.
- Suggested books: *Speech and Language Processing* (Jurafsky & Martin).
- Suggested courses: Intro NLP.
- Success criteria: Frames text-based research questions with measurable outcomes.

## 10. Quantitative Research
- Goals: Integrate all prior stages into a coherent research workflow.
- Topics: Experimental design, evidence synthesis, reproducibility.
- Suggested books: *Advances in Financial Machine Learning* (de Prado) for methodology critique.
- Suggested courses: Quant research seminars.
- Success criteria: Produces replicable research artifacts with clear decision relevance.
